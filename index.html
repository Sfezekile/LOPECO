BUILDING PEACE, ONE COMMUNITY AT A TIME.

The Lady of Peace Community Foundation (LOPECO) champions peacebuilding, honours everyday community heroes and empowers young people to lead change across Gauteng and beyond.

Our purpose

Peace is built by people doing the work and we recognise those on the front lines, create platforms for youth voices, and push for peace-centred responses to the social and environmental challenges South Africans face every day.

Community Recognition

We honour community actors working to end gender-based violence and femicide (GBVF), so their work is seen, valued and supported.

Environmental Peacebuilding

We advocate for peace-centred climate action, including environmental education led by community peace clubs.

Youth Desk

A seat at the table for young people, building leadership, dialogue and peace-making skills for the next generation.

Our story

Rooted in community, led by purpose.

Established in 2017, the Lady of Peace Community Foundation is a South African non-profit that believes lasting peace begins with ordinary people doing extraordinary work in their own neighbourhoods.

The foundation is led by Celeste Diale, President and Concept Developer, who heads the Youth Desk. In partnership with UN Women’s South Africa Multi-Country Office, LOPECO has recognised grassroots leaders across Gauteng who are working so that women and girls can live free from violence.
